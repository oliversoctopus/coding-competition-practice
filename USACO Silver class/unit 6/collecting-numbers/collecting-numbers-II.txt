You are given an array that contains each number between 1...n exactly once. Your task is to collect the numbers in multiple rounds.

In each round, you go through the array from left to right and collect as many numbers as possible. The numbers collected in each round must be increasing order. 

What is the min total number of rounds to collect all the integers 1...n?

Input (from terminal / stdin)

	The first line has an integer n: the array size, 1<=n<=2e5.

	The next line has n integers x[1], x[2], ..., x[n]: the numbers in the array.

Output (to terminal / stdout)

	Print one integer: the min number of rounds needed.


Sample input:
5
1 3 5 2 4

Sample output:
2

Note: In round 1, integers 1, 3, and 5 are collected. In round 2, integers 2, 4 are collected.
